# red-flag-scanner launch video plan

**Angle:** A scanner with no AI, no APIs and total confidence in its verdict.
**Format:** 20 seconds, 1920x1080, 30fps, 120 BPM, original soundtrack, cuts on the beat.

| Time | Type | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1.5s | title | UPLOAD A PHOTO. |
| 1.5-3s | title | SCAN A PROFILE. |
| 3-4.5s | title | GET ROASTED. |
| 4.5-11s | roast | RED FLAGS FOUND |
| 11-14s | title | NO AI. NO REAL APIS. JUST VIBES. |
| 14-16.5s | title | THIS IS SATIRE. WE MAKE IT UP. |
| 16.5-20s | outro | RED FLAG SCANNER |

## Source and stand-ins
- The roast lines are real ones from server/src/data/red-flags.json. No photos, faces or usernames are shown.

Public sites were loaded signed out with /api, /v1 and third-party requests blocked. No secrets or .env files were read.

## Check before posting
- Pointing a roast tool at other people's profiles could upset someone, even as satire. Keep the disclaimer if you post it.
- "No idea who you are" in the share copy is new wording. Check it against what the app stores (the README lists SQLite and shareable links).
